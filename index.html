Leadership

Built by someone who started at 12.

Raj Mehta built his first battery-powered bicycle at age 12, founded Raj Electromotives at 14, and secured an import-export license at 17 — reportedly among the youngest in India to do so. He founded Greta Electric Scooters in June 2019 as a subsidiary of Raj Electromotives Pvt. Ltd., while completing his Bachelor's degree in Automobile Engineering alongside running the company. His story has been widely covered in Indian business and EV press as one of the youngest founders in the country's automotive sector.
